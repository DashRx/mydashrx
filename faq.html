Does DashRx provide drivers?

Yes. DashRx provides drivers and prescription delivery service for pharmacies.

Can we schedule recurring deliveries?

Yes. Discuss scheduled routes, on-demand deliveries, or a mix of both with our team.

What must we enter before pickup?

For every delivery, your pharmacy must enter the patient’s name, delivery address, and phone number in the client portal before DashRx picks up.

DashRx covers Wayne, Oakland, and Macomb counties. Use the ZIP checker to check a delivery area. ZIP codes that cross a county boundary need address confirmation.
