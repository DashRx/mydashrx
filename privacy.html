WEBSITE PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

This notice describes the handling of information submitted through the DashRx public website. Patient information entered into the client portal is outside the scope of these public website forms.

Information you choose to provide

Quote requests include your pharmacy and contact details, pickup ZIP code, delivery volume, preferred schedule, and delivery locations. Driver applications include your contact details, city and ZIP code, preferred delivery area, availability, driving-related answers, optional experience, and an optional résumé.

How the information is used

DashRx uses quote information to respond to your inquiry and discuss delivery services. Application information is used to review your interest in driving opportunities and contact you about next steps. Submitting a form does not enroll you in marketing communications.

Form processing and email

This website sends submitted forms through FormSubmit to DashRx departmental inboxes. Quotes are directed to partnerships@mydashrx.com and driver applications, including attached résumés, to careers@mydashrx.com. FormSubmit, email providers, and hosting providers process information as needed to provide their services. Their services may also process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, and security logs.

Review FormSubmit’s privacy terms for its processing practices.

Patient information belongs in the client portal

Do not include patient names, patient addresses, patient phone numbers, prescriptions, medical information, government identity numbers, or payment details in public website forms or résumés. Before every pickup, pharmacies must enter the patient’s name, delivery address, and phone number directly into the client portal for that delivery.

Local interactions and other sites

The ZIP checker evaluates a ZIP code in the browser; using the checker alone does not submit that ZIP to DashRx. This website does not include advertising trackers or a marketing analytics integration. The separately operated client portal and form-processing pages may have their own notices and technologies.

Access, correction, and deletion requests

Contact support@mydashrx.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted. DashRx may need to verify your request. Some records may need to be retained for legal, contractual, security, or operational reasons. For retention questions, contact support@mydashrx.com.

Safeguards and contact

Use only the information needed for your inquiry or application. No website or email system can guarantee absolute security. Questions about this notice can be sent to support@mydashrx.com or discussed by calling (313) 333-2133.

Changes

Updates to this notice will be posted here with a revised effective date.
