WEBSITE INFORMATION

Website Terms

Effective October 20, 2026

About this website

The DashRx website provides information about pharmacy delivery services, lets pharmacies request quotes, and allows prospective drivers to express interest in opportunities. Website information does not replace a signed service agreement or driver agreement.

Quotes and delivery arrangements

A quote request is an inquiry. It does not create a delivery booking, guarantee availability, or establish pricing. Delivery timing, pricing, responsibilities, and other service requirements are confirmed separately with DashRx.

Coverage checker

DashRx’s stated delivery area includes Wayne, Oakland, and Macomb counties. The ZIP checker uses published geographic reference data. ZIP codes may cross county lines, and reference data can change. When a ZIP spans covered and uncovered areas, the delivery address’s county must be confirmed with dispatch before arranging service.

Client portal and pickup preparation

Before pickup, pharmacies must enter the patient’s name, delivery address, and phone number in the client portal for every delivery. Public website forms are for business inquiries and driver applications; do not enter patient or prescription information in them. Access to the client portal is subject to the portal’s own access requirements and applicable agreements.

Driver applications

An application or résumé expresses interest and does not guarantee engagement, employment, a specific route, schedule, or compensation. Role requirements and any additional checks are discussed separately. Provide accurate information and submit only documents you are entitled to share.

Use of website forms

Use the forms for legitimate inquiries and applications. Do not submit harmful files, spam, another person’s confidential information, or patient information. Résumé attachments are limited to PDF, DOC, or DOCX files up to 5 MB.

Third-party services

Form processing, email delivery, and the client portal rely on third-party services. These services have their own terms and privacy practices. A link to another site does not change the terms of a separate DashRx agreement.

Questions
